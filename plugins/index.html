Scripts for REAPER that make the daily work faster

Emelowsky Plugins are ReaScripts made by a sound engineer who has worked in REAPER for over 15 years. Every script solves one thing that got in the way in real sessions: finding plugins, keeping the mix balance in groups, comping multitrack recordings, knowing where in the song you are.
