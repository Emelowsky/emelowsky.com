Session Styler for REAPER

Every session in your colours and names - one click, or automatically when the project opens.

A session from another studio comes in with grey tracks and names your controller cannot show. Session Styler colours tracks, folders, items, markers and regions by your rules, shortens and centres the names for your control surface display and sets layouts, icons and heights - every time the same way.

  • Colour rules by track name: exact, starts with, contains, ends with
  • Child tracks get lighter shades of their folder colour
  • Names shortened and centred for controller displays (iCON, Mackie)
  • Layout, icon, height and folder state per rule
  • Imports your SWS Auto Color rules
  • Preview before applying, one undo step, optional run on project open

Select Link for REAPER

Click an item and its track is selected. Click a track and its items are selected.

In REAPER item selection and track selection live separate lives - you pick a vocal take, then still have to click the right track before you add an FX or change the routing. Select Link keeps both in step, in both directions, quietly in the background.

  • Item selected - its track is selected too, and the other way round
  • Ctrl and Shift work just like in REAPER: add, remove, ranges
  • Grab a group of selected items and move it - the selection stays
  • A track selects all of its items or only those inside the time selection
  • On/off from the toolbar, settings in a small Options window
  • Free
