Emelowsky Plugins / Fast Group

Fast Group for REAPER

Track grouping for REAPER that keeps your mix balance - even at the end of the fader.

Get the free demo Watch the overview Forum thread

What it does

Push a grouped fader to the top and bring it back: with REAPER's native groups the volume differences between your tracks are gone. Fast Group keeps every volume and pan offset, even when a fader hits the end of its range and after you reopen the project.

  • Volume and pan offsets stay, also at the limits of the fader
  • Offsets survive closing and reopening the project
  • Create a group from the selected tracks in one click
  • Group colours and badges on the tracks, group templates
  • Popup window for quick group actions

Free demo: everything works with up to 2 active groups at a time.

Install

Gumroad: download the zip from the product page and follow the quick start guide inside.

ReaPack: Extensions > ReaPack > Import repositories, paste https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emelowsky/reapack/main/index.xml, then find Fast Group in Browse packages.

Requires: REAPER 6 or 7 (Windows, macOS, Linux) and the free ReaImGui extension (ReaPack).
