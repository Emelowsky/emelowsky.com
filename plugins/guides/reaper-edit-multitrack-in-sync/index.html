Edit multitrack recordings in REAPER without losing phase: cut, move and trim every mic together

Editing multitrack drums is mostly about one rule: whatever you do to one mic, you do to all of them. Move a hit on the snare track only, and the overheads now play it a few milliseconds earlier - the sound gets phasey and smaller.

In REAPER you can select items on many tracks and edit them together, and item grouping helps to keep them selected. The risk is in the small moments: one item not selected, one split that missed a track, one trim done with the wrong selection.

Edits that always follow

Individual Take Sync Group for REAPER keeps the items you recorded together linked. Work on any of them, and the rest of the group follows automatically:

  • Split on one track - every track is split at the same sample
  • Move a part - all parts move by exactly the same amount
  • Trim or delete - the whole group is trimmed or deleted
  • Optional per group: item volume, pan, mute, phase and fades
  • Pause a group for a moment when you really want to edit a single mic

It is just as useful for doubled guitars, stacked vocals or a live band recorded to many tracks - anything that was recorded at the same time and has to stay together.
