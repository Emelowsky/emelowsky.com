The same track groups in every REAPER session: group templates

If you mix a lot, most sessions need the same groups: drums, toms, overheads, bass DI and amp, rhythm guitars, lead vocal and doubles, backing vocals. Building them again for every song is a few minutes each time - and with an album of twelve songs, it adds up.

A full REAPER project template helps when the tracks are always the same. But sessions from other studios come with their own track names and order, so the template does not fit and the groups have to be made by hand anyway.

Templates for the groups, not the whole project

Fast Group for REAPER lets you save your groups as templates - the name, the colour and the settings of each group - and apply them again in the next session instead of building every group from scratch.

  • Group templates for your typical setups: drums, guitars, vocals, stems
  • Quick add (Ctrl+G) for a one-off group from the selected tracks
  • Colours and badges on the tracks, so you see the groups at a glance
  • Volume and pan offsets that stay - also at the end of the fader and after reopening the project
  • A Popup window under its own shortcut for quick group actions

Combined with a script that puts delivered tracks into your template, like Track Drop, a new session can be ready to mix in a few minutes.
