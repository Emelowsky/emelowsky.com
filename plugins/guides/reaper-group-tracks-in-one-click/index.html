How to group tracks in REAPER in one click (with mute, solo and arm in sync)

Doubled guitars, a stereo pair of overheads, three backing vocals - in almost every session there are tracks that should always move together. In REAPER you can link them with the track grouping settings, where you choose a group and tick which parameters follow (volume, pan, mute, solo and so on). It is powerful, but setting it up for every new pair of tracks takes a while.

The quick way

Fast Group for REAPER turns this into one step: select the tracks and press Ctrl+G (or click Quick add). The group is ready, with a colour and a badge on the tracks, so you always see what belongs together.

  • Mute, solo, record arm and polarity simply match across the group
  • Reverse Pan mirrors the pan of a double-tracked part: pan one guitar left, the other goes right
  • Pause the sync for a moment to adjust one track, then resume - the group remembers the new offset
  • Save your usual setups (drums, guitars, vocals) as group templates and apply them in the next session
  • Volume and pan offsets stay even when a fader hits the end of its range

If you already have groups you like, a Popup window under its own shortcut gives you quick group actions without leaving the arrange view.
