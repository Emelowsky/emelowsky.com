How to link track and item selection in REAPER

In REAPER the items you select and the tracks you select live separate lives. You marquee a few parts, open the effects... and they land on the track you clicked minutes ago. Or you click a track to move its items - and they are not selected.

If you came from another DAW where selecting a clip also selects its track, this catches you out again and again.

Keeping both selections together

Select Link for REAPER is a free script that links track and item selection in both directions, quietly in the background:

  • Select items (click or marquee) - their tracks get selected
  • Click a track - all of its items get selected, or only those inside the time selection
  • Ctrl and Shift work as usual; grab a selected group and drag it - the selection stays
  • Each direction can be switched off on its own; on/off from a toolbar button

It is free, with no license and no limits. If you like how it works, have a look at the other Emelowsky Plugins scripts.
