How to insert FX chains with one click in REAPER

Most engineers start their vocal, drum bus or guitar chains from the same few setups. REAPER lets you save the FX of a track as an FX chain and load it again later from the FX chain list - a great feature that many people forget they have, because the chains are one more list to dig through.

Chains next to your plugins

Plugin Shelf for REAPER puts FX chains on the same shelf as your plugins, as tiles. One click inserts the whole chain on the selected track.

  • FX chains as tiles, in your own categories ("Vocals", "Drum bus", "Mastering")
  • Plugins with a chosen preset as separate tiles - "Compressor - gentle vocal", "Compressor - parallel smash"
  • Every tile has its own REAPER action, so a chain can sit under a shortcut or a toolbar button
  • Search across plugins and chains by word fragments in any order

A tip: save a "starting point" chain for every type of track you mix often. With one click per track, setting up a new session takes minutes instead of half an hour.
