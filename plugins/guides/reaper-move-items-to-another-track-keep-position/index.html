Move recordings onto the tracks of your template in REAPER - without shifting them in time

Your template already has its tracks, each with plugins, sends and colours. The new recordings come in on their own tracks, and you want them on the template tracks instead - in exactly the same position in time.

Dragging items to a track far down a big template is fiddly: you scroll while holding the mouse, and one small sideways move shifts the recording in time. With many items on many tracks it is easy to miss one.

Only the track changes

Item Drop for REAPER moves the selected items onto any track with one click in its list:

  • Position, length and every take stay exactly the same
  • Items from several tracks move in one go
  • Empty template tracks are easy to spot in the list
  • Find the target by word fragments in any order
  • The last target stays at the top: press Enter to send the next selection there again
  • Every move is one undo step

If you would rather move whole tracks into the folders of your template, have a look at its sister script Track Drop.
