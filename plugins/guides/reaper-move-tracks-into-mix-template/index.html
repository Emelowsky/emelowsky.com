How to put delivered tracks into your mix template in REAPER

Many engineers mix on their own template: folders for drums, bass, guitars and vocals, with buses, sends and plugins already in place. The tracks you get from a client arrive in a different order and with different names, and the first half hour of every mix goes into moving them where they belong.

In REAPER you can drag tracks in the track panel into a folder. With a long template that means scrolling up and down with a handful of tracks, dropping them in exactly the right spot, and not accidentally nesting a folder inside another one.

One click per move

Track Drop for REAPER shows every track of your project in a small window, with folder indents and colours. Select tracks in REAPER, click the target in the list, and they drop right into place:

  • Click a folder - the selected tracks go to its end
  • Click a normal track - it becomes a new folder with the selected tracks inside
  • Folders move with everything inside; the rest of the template keeps its structure
  • Find any target by word fragments in any order ("tom" finds TOMS)
  • The last target stays at the top: press Enter to send the next selection there again
  • Every move is one undo step

Its sister script Item Drop does the same for items, when your template tracks are already set up and you only need the recordings on them.
