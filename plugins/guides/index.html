REAPER guides

Short answers to everyday REAPER problems from mixing and editing sessions - what is going on, what you can do in REAPER itself, and how our scripts make it faster.

  • Why REAPER track groups lose your volume balance - and how to keep it
    Push a grouped fader in REAPER to the top and bring it back - the level differences between your tracks are gone. Here is why it happens and how to keep every offset.
  • How to group tracks in REAPER in one click (with mute, solo and arm in sync)
    Make a group from the selected tracks in REAPER with one keystroke, and keep mute, solo, record arm and polarity in sync across the group.
  • How to mirror the pan of double-tracked guitars in REAPER
    Pan one guitar left and its double goes right by the same amount - in REAPER, with one group. Keep the stereo image even while you adjust the width.
  • The same track groups in every REAPER session: group templates
    Stop rebuilding drum, guitar and vocal groups for every song. Save your REAPER track groups as templates and apply them to a new session in one click.
  • How to comp multitrack drums in REAPER: switch the take on every mic at once
    Recorded drums on many mics in several takes? Switch the take on one mic in REAPER and let every mic follow - playlist-style comping without losing phase.
  • Edit multitrack recordings in REAPER without losing phase: cut, move and trim every mic together
    Cut, move and trim multitrack drums or doubled parts in REAPER on every track at once, sample accurate, so the microphones stay in phase.
  • A faster way to find plugins in REAPER: a visual plugin browser
    Hundreds of plugins in REAPER's FX list? Use a visual shelf of plugin tiles with automatic categories and search by word fragments - one click puts the plugin on the track.
  • How to insert FX chains with one click in REAPER
    Save your favourite FX chains in REAPER and put a whole chain on a track with one click - from a visual shelf of tiles, together with your plugins and presets.
  • How to put delivered tracks into your mix template in REAPER
    Got a session to mix and your own REAPER template? Send the delivered tracks into the right folders of the template with one click, without breaking its layout.
  • Move recordings onto the tracks of your template in REAPER - without shifting them in time
    Move items to another track in REAPER with one click: position, length and every take stay the same, only the track changes. Ideal for mix templates.
  • How to link track and item selection in REAPER
    In REAPER item selection and track selection are separate. Link them: click an item and its track is selected, click a track and its items are selected - free script.
