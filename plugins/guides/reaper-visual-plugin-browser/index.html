A faster way to find plugins in REAPER: a visual plugin browser

After a few years with REAPER most of us have hundreds of plugins: several EQs, compressors, reverbs from different developers, plus the ones that came in bundles. REAPER's Add FX window lists all of them, with folders and a search field - fine when you know the exact name, slow when you are looking for "that compressor I used last week".

Seeing your plugins instead of reading them

Plugin Shelf for REAPER shows your plugins as a shelf of tiles, each with its icon. Click a tile and the plugin lands on the selected track or item.

  • The shelf fills itself in seconds: automatic categories (EQ, Dynamics, Reverb...) or a copy of your REAPER folders
  • Over 4000 plugin icons from 130+ developers; missing one? capture it straight from the plugin window
  • Search by word fragments in any order: "comp air" finds AIR Compressor
  • Your own categories and order - drag tiles like on a phone
  • Plugin Shelf Override can open the shelf instead of REAPER's Add FX window
  • Every plugin gets its own REAPER action - for shortcuts, toolbar buttons, MIDI controllers or a Stream Deck

The free demo works without a time limit with 4 categories of 5 plugins each - enough to try it on a real session.
