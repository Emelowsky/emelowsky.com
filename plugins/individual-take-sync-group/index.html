Emelowsky Plugins / Individual Take Sync Group

Individual Take Sync Group for REAPER

Playlist-style comping for multitrack recordings in REAPER: switch one take, the whole group follows.

Get the free demo Watch the overview Forum thread

What it does

Comping drums in REAPER means switching the take on every mic by hand. With Individual Take Sync Group you switch the take on one drum mic and every mic in the group switches with it. Splits, moves, trims and deletes follow too, so the phase between the mics never drifts.

  • Switch the take on one track - all tracks in the group switch at once
  • Splits, moves, trims and deletes follow across the group
  • Phase between the mics stays intact
  • Works like playlists in Pro Tools, inside REAPER
  • Several independent groups (drums, vocals, guitars)

Free demo: 2 groups with up to 6 tracks each.

Install

Gumroad: download the zip from the product page and follow the quick start guide inside.

ReaPack: Extensions > ReaPack > Import repositories, paste https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emelowsky/reapack/main/index.xml, then find Individual Take Sync Group in Browse packages.

Requires: REAPER 6 or 7 (Windows, macOS, Linux) and the free ReaImGui extension (ReaPack).
